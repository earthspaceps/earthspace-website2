EarthSpace Project Solutions LLP is a multidisciplinary project development and construction management firm based in Kerala. We exist to close the gap between design intent and on-site reality.

Unlike traditional contractors or standalone design firms, we operate as a single-point solution partner. We integrate architectural vision with engineering rigour and construction management from day one. This integrated model eliminates fragmentation, reduces risk, and ensures that every project is delivered on time, within budget, and to the highest quality standards.
